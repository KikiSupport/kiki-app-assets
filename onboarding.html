Setup Checklist
1

Connect Slack

Link your Slack workspace so client messages flow into Kiki

  1. Click here to connect Slack — you'll be taken to Slack to authorise Kiki
  2. Find Slack and click Connect
  3. Sign in to your Slack workspace and authorise Kiki
  4. You'll be redirected back to Kiki once connected
💡 Kiki needs access to your Slack workspace to read and send messages on your behalf. Your clients will see messages from you, not from a bot.
2

Add Kiki to a Slack Channel

Invite the Kiki bot to each client channel you want to manage

  1. Open the Slack channel you use with a client
  2. Type /invite @Kiki and press enter
  3. Kiki will send you a direct message with a link to link the channel to a contact
  4. Click the link, select the client contact from the list, and click Link Channel
📌 Repeat this for every client channel you want to manage inside Kiki. Each channel maps to one contact.
3

Connect WhatsApp

Add your WhatsApp Business number to receive messages in Kiki

  1. Go to Settings → Phone System → WhatsApp
  2. Click Connect WhatsApp
  3. Follow the steps to verify your WhatsApp Business number
  4. Once connected, messages from WhatsApp will appear in your Conversations inbox
💬 WhatsApp messages are handled natively by Kiki. No extra setup needed once connected.
4

Send Your First Message

Reply to a client from Kiki across any channel

You can now message clients from within Kiki across all connected channels.

Slack
WhatsApp
✉️ Email
  1. Go to Conversations in the left sidebar
  2. Find the contact you want to message
  3. To message via Slack, click the email dropdown at the bottom and select Slack, then choose the channel
  4. Type your message and hit send. It'll arrive in Slack as a message from you
  5. For WhatsApp and email, just use the standard message composer
✨ When a client replies on Slack or WhatsApp, it automatically appears in your Kiki inbox as a new message. No switching apps needed.
🎉

You're all set!

Kiki is ready to go. Head to Conversations to start managing your client messages.
