Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up your Kiki account
0 of 5 steps completed
Setup checklist
1

Install Kiki

Connect your account so Kiki can access your data

  1. Open the Kiki install link sent to you by your account manager
  2. Select your sub-account from the dropdown
  3. Click Allow & Install
  4. You'll be redirected back to Kiki once connected
This step authorises Kiki to access your conversations, contacts, calendar, invoices, and email. You only need to do this once.
2

Connect Slack

Link your Slack workspace so client messages appear in Kiki

  1. Click here to connect Slack — you'll be taken to Slack to authorise Kiki
  2. Sign in to your Slack workspace and click Allow
  3. You'll be redirected back to Kiki once connected
Kiki needs access to your Slack workspace to read and send messages. Your clients will see messages from you, not a bot.
3

Add Kiki to your client channels

Invite the Kiki bot to each Slack channel you want to manage

  1. Open the Slack channel you share with a client
  2. Type /invite @Kiki and press Enter
  3. Kiki will send you a direct message with a link to link the channel to a contact
  4. Click the link, find the client in the list, and click Link Channel
Repeat this for every client channel. Each channel links to one contact record.
4

Set up your sending email

Connect your own domain so emails land in inboxes, not spam

  1. Go to Settings → Email Services
  2. Click Add Sending Domain and enter your domain (e.g. mail.yourbusiness.com)
  3. Add the DNS records shown to your domain provider and click Verify
  4. Once verified, set it as your default sending domain
We strongly recommend connecting your own domain. Until you do, emails will send from slack.wearekiki.com which may land in junk folders while the domain warms up.
If you don't have a custom domain yet, contact us and we can set up a dedicated Kiki subdomain for you.
5

Invite your team

Give your team access so they can manage conversations too

  1. Go to Settings → My Staff
  2. Click Add Employee and enter their name and email
  3. Set their role and click Save
  4. They'll receive a login invite via email
Each team member gets their own login. You can control what they can see and do from their role settings.
🎉

You're all set

Kiki is ready to go. Head to Messages to start managing your client conversations.
